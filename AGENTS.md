# Replay the Spire / 回放尖塔

Maintain this independent GPL-3.0-only Slay the Spire 2 utility. Preserve the English and Chinese names. Use small, scoped Conventional Commits on a clean build. Read README.md and docs/validation.md before changing native integration.

Single-player recording and faithful native replay precede gallery, video, practice branches and multiplayer. Prefer game action events and compact room-based storage. Keep recording independent of frame capture. Test with local SDKs, an isolated profile, Steam disabled and audio muted. Do not commit or redistribute game assemblies, extracted sources, saves or generated gameplay media.
